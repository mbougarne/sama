# Community conduct

Sama welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, give specific technical feedback, and help make collaboration clear and constructive.

Harassment, discriminatory attacks, threats, disclosure of private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Critique ideas and implementation choices without attacking the person behind them. Respect requests to stop an interaction.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They should explain decisions privately where possible and handle reports with discretion. Reports involving a maintainer should be reviewed by another maintainer when one is available.

Until the public repository and moderation contacts are established, report concerns privately to the repository owner through an existing channel. A dedicated reporting route is a prerequisite for public community launch.
