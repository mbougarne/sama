# Community conduct

Sama is a technical project. Its shared spaces exist to improve the software, documentation, security, reliability, and user experience. Participation is based on relevant contributions and observable conduct, not a person's culture, background, beliefs, or affiliations.

## Keep discussion within the project

Keep issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions focused on Sama. Do not bring personal beliefs, political campaigning, debates about race, or unrelated ideological arguments into these spaces. Contributors do not need to disclose or defend their personal views.

Product requirements, accessibility, localization, security, licensing, and concrete conduct reports are relevant when they directly affect the project or participation. This rule must not be used to silence a report of threats, discriminatory attacks, or other prohibited behavior.

## Discuss the work, not the person

Be direct and specific about a technical problem, the evidence, and the change needed. Disagreement and concise communication are welcome. Contributors communicate across languages and cultures; do not infer hostility from brevity, translation, an accent, or unfamiliar phrasing. Ask for clarification when meaning is uncertain.

Do not label another person's character, manners, intelligence, upbringing, parents, or family. Moderation must follow the same rule: identify the exact statement or action and the rule it breaks rather than assigning a personality label. For example, say “This comment contains a personal insult; please rewrite it around the technical issue.”

Threats, harassment, discriminatory attacks, disclosure of private information, personal insults, and deliberate disruption are prohibited. Cultural differences do not excuse those actions, and moderation must be based on evidence rather than assumptions about a person's background.

## Work within the project's direction

The Go backend, React + TypeScript frontend, Webpack bundler, and PostgreSQL foundation are fixed project choices. Language, framework, or bundler replacement campaigns are out of scope. Improvements, bug reports, performance work, and security/version updates within the chosen stack are welcome.

Maintainers may decline changes that conflict with the project's scope or accepted architecture. If this direction or these participation rules do not suit you, you are free to maintain a fork under the project's license or choose another tool. The project is not obliged to adopt a fork's direction.

## Moderation

Maintainers should identify the relevant content, explain the rule, and request a concrete correction when practical. Repeated disruption can lead to a locked discussion or restricted participation. Serious threats, harassment, or disclosure of private information may require immediate action. Apply the rules consistently across roles and cultures.

Until public reporting contacts are established, send concerns privately to the repository owner through an existing channel. Reports involving a maintainer should be handled by another maintainer when available. Keep reports and personal information private; do not turn moderation into a public argument about someone's character.

These rules govern participation in Sama's project spaces. They do not add restrictions to the software license or revoke anyone's licensed right to use, modify, or redistribute the software.
